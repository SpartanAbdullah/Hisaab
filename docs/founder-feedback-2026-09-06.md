#### How discovery works (plain language — English here; needs a `ur` version before it ships as an in-app help sheet)

- **It is already on by default.** The moment you save a number under Settings → My phone number, Hisaab stores it in international form (+971…, +92…) and switches "Let people with my number find me" on in the same step. The switch only appears once a number exists.
- **Who can find you.** Only someone who has ALREADY typed your exact number into their own Hisaab contacts sees that you are on Hisaab. Nobody can search by number, browse a list, or learn a number they did not already have.
- **What leaves the phone.** Hisaab never reads the address book and never asks for the contacts permission. The only numbers involved are the ones a user typed into Hisaab themselves — their own, and their contacts'. Contacts are already stored with the account, so the "is this person on Hisaab?" check is the server comparing numbers it already holds. Local-style numbers are understood for UAE and Pakistan; for any other country, type the country code.
- **What the finder sees.** Next to a matching contact: "This number is on Hisaab — Ali" with "Link them", marked not verified. They never see your number (they had it) and nothing else from your account.
- **What you must approve.** Nothing links automatically. You get an Inbox card — "Ali added you — Add / Not now", plus report and block. Only Add connects you both. *(Today the card and the push notification say "used your Hisaab code" even for a phone match — being fixed.)*
- **Limits.** 20 lookups per hour per user, 60 numbers per lookup; a failed link attempt counts too. If the limit is hit the app quietly stops checking until you reopen it.
- **Turning it off.** Settings → My phone number → switch off. Your number stays on file but nobody can find you, and someone who saw the hint earlier can no longer link. "Remove" deletes the number. Existing links are not undone — unlink from the contact.
- **"Verified" means nothing yet.** Hisaab does not check who owns a number (no SMS code; `docs/phone-auth.md` is parked). That is why the hint says "not verified — confirm with them before recording money".
